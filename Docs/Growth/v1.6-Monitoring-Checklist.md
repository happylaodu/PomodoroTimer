## 🚨 Alert Thresholds

### Critical Issues (Act Immediately)
- Crash rate >1%
- App Store rating drops below 4.0
- Major bug affecting >10% of users
- Security/privacy concern raised

### Warning Signals (Monitor Closely)
- Download trend declining >20% vs baseline
- Negative review spike (>3 in one day)
- Retention rate drops >10%
- Crash rate >0.5%

### Positive Signals (Amplify)
- 5-star reviews mentioning achievements
- Social media viral post (>100 engagements)
- Press/blog coverage
- Sudden download spike

## 🎯 Success Definition

**v1.6 is considered successful if**:

✅ **No Regressions**:
- Crash rate stays <0.5%
- Rating stays >=4.5
- No major feature breaks

✅ **Engagement Improves**:
- 7-day retention +5% or more
- Settings panel visits increase
- Daily active users increase

✅ **Achievement System Validated**:
- >=70% of reviews mention achievements positively
- >=50% of users unlock >=3 achievements
- Minimal complaints about distraction

✅ **Growth Metrics**:
- Downloads >=v1.5 average
- GitHub stars +10
- Social media engagement positive
